# VISION: the feel we are chasing

Priority: this file defines the GAMEPLAY FEEL. Every feature is judged against it.

## Owner's directive (summary)
Many players cannot buy Sniper Elite on Steam, or cannot run it because it needs
a powerful PC. One Shot exists so those players get the SAME thrill, tension, and
competitiveness in a web browser, on modest hardware and on phones.
A player who cannot play Sniper Elite should be able to play One Shot instead and feel
the same excitement.

## What "like Sniper Elite" means here
- YES: gameplay feel, rhythm, nerves, breath, tension, patience, competitiveness.
- NO: names, art, maps, models, sounds, UI, story, or the original's rules.
  Our rules are our own (RULES.md).
- Master prompt rule 9 still holds: everything is original. The reference name appears
  ONLY in internal docs. Never in the game, store pages, or marketing.

## Feel pillars (every feature must serve at least one)
1. One shot matters. Limited rounds, bolt cycle, and self-exposure make every
   trigger pull a real decision.
2. Breath and nerves. Breath-hold, lung meter, heartbeat, forced exhale.
   The player's calm IS the skill.
3. Patience and cat-and-mouse. Read the terrain, spot the glint, relocate after
   shooting, outwait the opponent.
4. Real ballistics you learn. Drop, wind, travel time. Skill grows with practice,
   never with purchases.
5. Sound is information. Delayed shot report (343 m/s), wind, heartbeat,
   distant cues. Sound tells you where danger is.
6. The payoff. The bullet-cam on a long lethal hit is the reward moment.
   It must feel earned and cinematic.
7. Exposure has a cost. Shooting, staying scoped too long, and camping all leak
   your position.
8. A tension curve. Quiet stalking, then sudden-death pulses, then one decisive shot.

## Weak hardware first
The audience includes people with weak PCs and phones. When visual fidelity
conflicts with a smooth frame rate on weak devices, frame rate wins.
The feel comes from mechanics, sound, and timing, not from expensive graphics.

## Feel gate (pending owner approval, see DECISIONS.md D-002)
At the end of phases 1, 2, 3, and 7 the owner answers one question:
"Does this feel as tense and thrilling as the reference?"
A "no" means we tune before moving on.
